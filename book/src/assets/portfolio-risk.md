# 14. Portfolio and risk

Catalog mapping: [§14 Portfolio and risk](../../../../../../../docs/finance-rs/catalog.md#14-portfolio-and-risk).

The `perf`, `stats`, `attribution`, and `factors` modules cover returns,
drawdowns, ratios, VaR/CVaR, regression, factor exposure, Brinson attribution,
and multi-period linking. Annualized functions take `periods_per_year`
explicitly so frequency is visible in every call.
