# 11. Private equity and LBO

Catalog mapping: [§11 Private equity / LBO](../../../../../../../docs/finance-rs/catalog.md#11-private-equity--lbo).

`fund` supplies fund multiples, PME variants, preferred-return schedules, and
European/American waterfalls. `corp::lbo` models debt paydown and sponsor
returns. Use dated cash flows when timing affects IRR.
