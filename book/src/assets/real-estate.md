# 10. Real estate

Catalog mapping: [§10 Real estate](../../../../../../../docs/finance-rs/catalog.md#10-real-estate).

The `re` module contains cap rate, cash-on-cash return, DSCR, GRM, debt yield,
LTV, and breakeven occupancy formulas. `amortization` covers mortgage payment
rows, extra principal, balloon and interest-only terms, PMI, and refinance
comparison.
