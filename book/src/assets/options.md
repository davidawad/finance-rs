# 5. Options and derivatives

Catalog mapping: [§5 Options and derivatives](../../../../../../../docs/finance-rs/catalog.md#5-options-and-derivatives).

`options` provides Black-Scholes-Merton, Black-76, Bachelier, FX, and Margrabe
pricing, Greeks, implied volatility, and realized-volatility estimators.
Inputs and no-arbitrage bounds are validated with typed errors.
