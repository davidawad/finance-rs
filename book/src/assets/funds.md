# 13. Funds

Catalog mapping: [§13 Funds](../../../../../../../docs/finance-rs/catalog.md#13-funds).

The `fund` module calculates TVPI, DPI, RVPI, MOIC, KS-PME, Long-Nickels,
PME+, mPME, direct alpha, pacing, look-through, and pooled waterfalls.
Properties such as `TVPI = DPI + RVPI` are tested as accounting identities.
