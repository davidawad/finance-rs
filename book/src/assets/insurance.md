# 15. Insurance, annuities and retirement

Catalog mapping: [§15 Insurance, annuities and retirement](../../../../../../../docs/finance-rs/catalog.md#15-insurance-annuities-and-retirement).

The feature-gated `actuarial` module provides annuity factors, life
contingencies, retirement withdrawals, Social Security adjustments, and IUL
crediting. Caller-supplied life tables are validated; the crate does not
invent mortality data.
